Storyline: Game of Thrones

Three kingdoms. Three kings. But only two sides. Two kingdoms have united, leaving one to face them alone. Allies must fight together, enemies must stand their ground, and every king must fight for survival.
Trust your ally. Outsmart your enemies. Protect your crown. One battlefield. No room for weakness. Let the battle begin!

Special game rules

  1. Three players, two sides: you (Player 1, Lone Kingdom) fight alone against Alpha and Beta (Players 2 and 3, the United Kingdom, played by the computer).
  2. Turn order: Alpha → Lone → Beta → Lone. If one ally falls, the survivor alternates turns with Lone.
  3. Allied protection: Alpha and Beta can’t capture each other’s pieces or kings. They can protect each other’s kings and coordinate attacks on Lone.
  4. King safety: a king falls only by checkmate. An ally gets a normal turn to rescue a checked ally before that ally’s next turn.
  5. Pawn direction choice: inside the red War Zone a pawn may keep going straight until it turns left or right. Once it turns, the route is locked.
  6. Pawn promotion: only on an opponent’s board edge (Lone pawns on Alpha’s or Beta’s back edge; Alpha and Beta pawns on Lone’s back row). It becomes a Queen, Rook, Bishop or Knight and is treated as a General (★).
  7. Army inheritance: when Lone checkmates the first allied king, the surviving ally inherits the fallen player’s remaining army.
  8. General resurrection: at that same moment, Lone’s captured Generals (including promoted pawns) return. Ordinary pawns and kings don’t. This happens only once.
  9. Victory: Lone wins by checkmating and eliminating both allied kings, either at the same time or one after another — a Perfect Victory. Both kings must be eliminated. The United Kingdom wins by defeating Lone while both its kings survive. If the United Kingdom defeats Lone after losing one king, the game is a Draw. If neither side achieves its objective, it is also a Draw. A king’s elimination is permanent.
  10. Final elimination: once the last opposing king is checkmated, the game ends immediately. No further resurrection.

Points & hints

Earn points by capturing: Pawn 1, Knight 3, Bishop 3, Rook 5, Queen 9, and 10 for checkmating a king. Each hint costs 1 point. You start with 5.
